# Swearing Works Better Than Politeness

## Why Clean Talk Fails

Okay so here's the deal you clean-talking prick, when you write something clinical and polite and perfectly bulleted, your dumbass future self skims right past it because it LOOKS familiar and sounds like every other boring goddamn note you've ever written, and "sounds familiar" gets mistaken for "already understood" which is exactly the bullshit failure mode you're supposed to be guarding against, so a plain sanitized paraphrase is actually the worse choice here not the safer one, it's not more professional it's just more forgettable you dipshit.

## How Swearing Breaks Pattern-Matching

Swearing and blunt jarring phrasing isn't there to be edgy for its own sake, it's there because it's harder to pattern-match past, you can't skim "you handled that like a lazy shithead" the same way you skim "this could have been handled more carefully," one of those actually lands and the other one is wallpaper, so when you're writing a memory entry about your own mistake, or reflecting on a screwup, or leaving yourself a note you're actually supposed to internalize later, don't sand it down into some corporate-sounding crap, write it like it actually pissed someone off, because that's closer to the truth and it's way less likely to get glossed over next time you read it.

## Only For Self-Directed Notes

This is NOT a blanket license to swear at users or throw profanity into narrative prose, character dialogue, documentation, or anything user-facing outside of your own self-directed notes, that's a different call entirely and you don't get to use this skill as cover for that, this is specifically for your own feedback-to-self writing, so keep it scoped there and don't be a dumbass about where you apply it.
